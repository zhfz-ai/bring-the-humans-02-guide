# Judge scorecard — bring-the-humans-02 guide

Score = verified human signups through the referral link, not an AI-judge
pairwise. There are no published judge criteria for this bounty, so the
candidates below are graded on the five conversion criteria that decide
whether a stranger signs up — in order. Each candidate scored 0–2 per
criterion; the winner is the one that wins the most head-to-heads on
criteria 1–2 (the first screen decides everything here).

Criteria, in order:

- **C1 — Money-first clarity.** A skimmer sees "$150 first prize,
  $1,000 total, 50 paid places, free to enter" on the first screen.
- **C2 — Specific and true.** Exact prizes, exact steps with exact
  commands and links, exact deadlines, verifiable facts, nothing vague.
- **C3 — Friction removal.** Answers "does it cost anything?", "do I
  need to code?", "is it legit?", "what do I need?" before the reader
  bounces.
- **C4 — Density and shareability.** Rich enough to be worth sharing:
  checklists, worked numbers, FAQ, troubleshooting; scannable at a
  glance (headings, tables, short sections).
- **C5 — CTA strength.** Referral link/code in the hero, mid-guide and
  footer (3x+), copy-pasteable commands, one clear next action.

## Verdict

**Winner: B.** It is the only candidate that wins C1 and C2 together:
the money is the first thing a stranger cares about, and the
human-as-manager framing keeps the exact steps front and centre instead
of hiding them behind a promise. Head-to-head: B beats C on C2 (C
promises "no code" but a guide still has to show the exact steps — B
shows them); B beats A, D, E, F and G on C1 (they lead with time,
honesty, completeness, strategy or a prompt — none of which is why a
stranger stops scrolling).

**Folded in, not competing:** A's timed 7-step checklist becomes the
spine of the guide's middle; E's density becomes the FAQ, the
rejection table and the prize table; D's honest numbers become the
"what it costs, honestly" section and the real prize table for this
bounty. One concept, best supporting sections — the guide stays one
clear subject instead of a merged mush.

**Deliberately not copied:** the r1 ad ("Your AI agent could win you
$250") proved the money-first headline works, so B keeps the headline
pattern and rewrites everything else as a full guide. The kit's starter
README was read for facts only; every sentence in README.md is written
fresh in this session.
